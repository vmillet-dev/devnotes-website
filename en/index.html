01 / The applicationVersion 1.2.0

Your snippets, your notes, your requests.

A desktop application for everything a developer keeps at hand: snippets with fields, Markdown notes, lists, calculations and drawings, with an API client and 32 tools beside them. Encrypted on your machine, no account.

Download for Windows 16.1 MBOther systems and formats
  • Free, under the GPL-3.0
  • No account
  • Works offline
  • Windows, macOS and Linux
  1. 1Three areas

    The notes, the 32 tools and the API client, in the same window.

  2. 2Spaces and folders

    Your notes filed by project, with tags, as a list or on a board.

  3. 3A search

    CtrlK reads titles, code, lists and tags, ignoring case and accents.

  4. 4Snippets with fields

    {{namespace}} is filled in when you copy, even from another application with CtrlAltP.

  5. 5Calculations

    One result a line, in euros, hours or bytes, redone as soon as a figure changes.

  6. 6Lists

    Their card shows where you stand, without opening the list.

14:20Diff tool

Staging and production differ

Staging answers over HTTP, production over HTTPS. Both files side by side: Diff shows the lines that changed and, within each line, what changed. Either side can come from a note, and the difference can become one.

Tools ›Diff+ 4 lines− 3 lines
